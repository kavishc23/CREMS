CDW / EXCESS CLAUSES 
By accepting Collision Damage Waiver (CDW), the renter’s liability for accidental damage 
may be reduced to the agreed excess shown on the Rental Agreement. 
The renter remains liable for the full excess amount and agrees to pay the excess 
immediately upon demand following an incident. 
If CDW is declined, the renter is responsible for the total cost of loss, theft, or damage to the 
vehicle and related third-party property, subject to applicable law. 
The limitation of liability does not apply where the renter breaches the rental agreement, 
including unauthorized drivers, reckless driving, driving under the influence of alcohol or 
drugs, illegal use, overloading, off-road use, water damage, underbody damage, overhead 
damage, or failure to report an accident. 
Glass damage, tyre damage, water damage, underbody damage and overhead damage may 
be excluded from CDW protection and remain the responsibility of the renter. 
The renter authorizes the rental company to charge any excess, damages, towing, recovery, 
administrative fees, and related costs to the nominated credit card or account.
