[Heading 1] CREMS | Feasibility Study
[Heading 2] Carpenters Rental & Equipment Management System, including a scope-extension study for Carpenters Shipping (Fiji)
[Heading 1] 1. Executive Summary
[NoStyle] CREMS is proposed as a modular, web-based platform to manage the complete rental and maintenance lifecycle of hireable assets across Carpenters Fiji. This study finds the approved two-division MVP — Carpenters Motors (vehicle rental) and Carptrac (equipment hire) — technically, economically and operationally feasible within the confirmed 12-week, four-person delivery window, provided scope is held to the current baseline. At the client's request, this study also examines extending CREMS to a third subsidiary, Carpenters Shipping (Fiji) hire services (bins, scaffolding, portable toilets). That extension is technically and economically strong — the architecture was designed for exactly this kind of growth — but is not feasible inside the current 12-week MVP without either extending the timeline, adding capacity, or descoping existing Motors/Carptrac work. The recommendation is to deliver the two-division MVP as planned and treat Carpenters Shipping as the priority candidate for an Increment 2.
[Heading 1] 2. Purpose and Background
[NoStyle] Carpenters Fiji currently manages rental and hire assets across its divisions using manual or disconnected processes, making it difficult to see real-time availability, asset condition, maintenance history and profitability in one place. CREMS is proposed to give Carpenters Motors and Carptrac a shared, configurable control point for registering assets, checking availability, managing customers and bookings, recording inspections, scheduling maintenance and reporting on revenue and cost at the asset level, while keeping each division's data and workflow separated by role, division and branch.
[Heading 1] 3. Problem Statement
[NoStyle] Without a shared system, each division tracks assets, bookings and maintenance independently, which risks double-booking, delayed maintenance, inconsistent pricing, and management having no single view of asset utilization or profitability across the business. The absence of a common, extensible platform also means every future division that wants similar capability has to build or buy its own solution rather than reuse an existing one.
[Heading 1] 4. Objectives
[List Paragraph] Give staff a single system to register, locate and track the status of rentable assets.
[List Paragraph] Remove double-booking and status-conflict risk through real-time availability checking.
[List Paragraph] Standardize the rental lifecycle: quotation, approval, agreement, pickup inspection, active hire, return inspection and invoicing.
[List Paragraph] Capture preventive and corrective maintenance with cost and downtime visibility per asset.
[List Paragraph] Provide management with dashboards and reports on utilization, revenue, cost and profitability.
[List Paragraph] Build the platform so that a future division can be configured into CREMS without redesigning core modules.
[Heading 1] 5. Current Approved Scope
[NoStyle] The MVP baseline confirmed with the client on 10 August 2026 covers two divisions:
[List Paragraph] Carpenters Motors — rental vehicles (passenger and commercial): registration, odometer, fuel, driver details, inspection and daily rates.
[List Paragraph] Carptrac — forklifts, cranes, excavators, generators and related hire equipment: engine hours, operator qualifications, overtime, safety checks and hourly/daily rates.
[NoStyle] Both divisions share the same modules — authentication and RBAC, organization (division/branch), customer management, asset management, rental management, maintenance management, and finance/reporting — configured with division-specific asset categories, attributes and rate units.

[Heading 2] 9.4  Recommendation
[NoStyle] Carpenters Shipping (Fiji) hire services is a strong architectural and economic fit for CREMS, and should be the first candidate considered once the Motors/Carptrac MVP is accepted — this is consistent with Risk R17, which already anticipates this exact scope question, and Risk R3, whose own mitigation names Shipping as the first item to descope if capacity is tight. Two options are presented for the client's decision:
[List Paragraph] Option A (Recommended) — Deliver Motors + Carptrac on the current 12-week baseline; scope, design and pilot Carpenters Shipping as a funded Increment 2 immediately after handover, reusing the same architecture. Indicative effort: 2–3 additional sprints given no new module class is required.
[List Paragraph] Option B — Bring Shipping into the current MVP by formally descoping a Should/Could item elsewhere in Motors or Carptrac. This requires the client to accept a reduced feature set in the other two divisions and must go through the existing Change Management Plan (PMP §7.0) before any sprint is re-planned.
[Heading 1] 10. Alternatives Considered
[List Paragraph] Continue with manual/spreadsheet-based tracking — rejected: does not solve double-booking, maintenance visibility or cross-division reporting, and does not scale to a third division.
[List Paragraph] Buy an off-the-shelf rental/fleet management product — rejected for the MVP: licensing cost is outside the academic project's cash ceiling, and off-the-shelf products are not pre-configured for Carpenters' specific divisions, approval stages or asset categories.
[List Paragraph] Build all three divisions (Motors, Carptrac, Shipping) concurrently — rejected: exceeds four-person/12-week capacity per Risk R3; would force Must-priority Motors/Carptrac work to compete with a third division for the same limited hours.
[List Paragraph] Phased rollout (this proposal) — accepted: delivers a working MVP on the confirmed baseline while keeping Shipping's clearly-scoped, low-technical-risk extension ready to fund as Increment 2.
[Heading 1] 11. Overall Recommendation
[NoStyle] Proceed with CREMS for Carpenters Motors and Carptrac on the confirmed 12-week baseline (10 August – 30 October 2026). The solution is technically sound, affordable within the academic project's cost ceiling, and operationally aligned with existing staff roles. Carpenters Shipping (Fiji) hire services is feasible and recommended as Increment 2, not as part of the current MVP, unless the client formally accepts the schedule and scope trade-off in Option B above.
[Heading 1] 12. Approval
[NoStyle] Approval of this feasibility study confirms agreement with the current MVP baseline and the recommended treatment of the Carpenters Shipping extension as Increment 2, pending the client's choice between Option A and Option B in Section 9.4.
