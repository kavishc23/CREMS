Description of project:
The Car and Rental Equipment Management System (CREMS) is a centralized web-based application designed to
streamline the management of vehicle and equipment rentals across multiple business locations. The system will provide
an end-to-end solution for managing rental assets, customer bookings, contracts, vehicle availability, equipment
allocation, inspections, maintenance schedules, invoicing, and reporting.
The application will enable staff to efficiently reserve vehicles and equipment, monitor real-time availability, track rental
history, and manage the complete rental lifecycle from booking through to return. It will also support inspection checklists,
damage reporting, fuel tracking, and preventive maintenance scheduling to maximize asset utilization and reduce
operational downtime.
A comprehensive dashboard will provide management with real-time insights into fleet utilization, equipment availability,
maintenance status, rental revenue, overdue rentals, and operational performance. The solution will improve operational
efficiency, reduce manual paperwork, enhance customer service, and provide accurate reporting for business decision-
making.
Objectives
✓ Develop a centralized web-based system for managing vehicle and equipment rentals across multiple branches.
✓ Automate the complete rental lifecycle including reservation, rental, return, extension, and closure.
✓ Provide real-time visibility of vehicle and equipment availability to prevent double bookings.
✓ Maintain complete asset information including registration details, service history, depreciation, inspections, and
maintenance schedules.
✓ Manage customer profiles, rental agreements, deposits, and rental history.
✓ Record pre-rental and post-rental inspections including damages, fuel levels, accessories, and condition reports.
✓ Generate dashboards and operational reports covering fleet utilization, revenue, maintenance, overdue rentals, and
equipment performance.
✓Maintain a comprehensive audit trail of all rental transactions and asset movements.
